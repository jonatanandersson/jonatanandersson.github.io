Jonatan Andersson

Postdoctoral researcher in economic history at Uppsala University.

I am an economic historian working on cities, migration, and labor markets using historical microdata. My research studies how urbanization, mobility, and structural change shaped individual life chances in the past.

I use large-scale historical individual-level data to study questions in economic history and urban economics. Much of my work focuses on rural-urban migration, occupational attainment, and the long-run effects of place and labor-market opportunities.

Research interests

Economic history, migration, urban economics, labor markets, inequality, historical microdata, and quantitative social science.

Selected publications

  • Did cities increase skills during industrialization? Journal of Urban Economics (with Jakob Molinder), 2025.
  • Ascending from the bottom rung: The labor market assimilation of rural-urban migrants in Sweden, 1880–1910 Explorations in Economic History, 2025.
  • Moving from opportunity: Intergenerational mobility of rural-urban return migrants in Sweden, 1890s–1940s Economic History Review, 2025.
